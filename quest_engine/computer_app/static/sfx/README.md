# Sound effects

Drop 16-bit WAV files here, named to match the `sfx`, `sfx_pass` and `sfx_fail`
fields in the content packs. Nothing here is required — a missing effect is
simply printed to the terminal instead, and the game carries on.

The packs currently ask for:

| File | Used by | Suggested sound |
|---|---|---|
| `roar.wav` | dragon: `intro`, `warcry`, `finale` | a big low dragon roar, 2–4 s |
| `alarm.wav` | space: `intro`, `warcry`, `finale` | station klaxon or proximity alert |

Keep them short. They play over the narration bed on a separate mixer channel,
so anything longer than a few seconds will still be going when the next
instruction starts.

Avoid anything with a sharp transient loud enough to trigger the wands'
microphones during a `CLAP` window. The engine goes silent before opening
microphone challenges, but an effect fired by the *previous* step can still be
ringing out.

Narration WAVs do not belong here. Those live in `static/voice/<pack>/` and are
generated by `tools/build_voice.py`.
